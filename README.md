# Rightmove-vs-Autotrader-analysis
Financial and business performance analysis: Rightmove vs Auto Trader (2023-2025)
## Company Overview
Rightmove, an online property portal based in the UK, connects home buyers, renters, and estate agents through property listings. It works as a subscription-based marketplace. Its revenue consists mainly of fees charged to estate agents and developers to advertise properties on their platform. A similar English company is Autotrader. It's an online automotive marketplace who connect car buyers with dealerships and private sellers. Similar to Rightmove's model, it adopts a subscription-based listing approach that bills dealers for listing their vehicles. It does not take a percentage of the sale. Both companies have a similar business model. They are asset-light high margin digital model and get revenue from fees and subscriptions rather than selling stuff. Hence they make for a good comparison. Despite being in different markets.
## Key Financial Trends
- **Revenue growth:** Rightmove's revenue jumped from £346.3m in 2023 to £425.1m in 2025, an increase of around 23%. From £500.2m (2023), Auto Trader revenue grew to £601.1m (2025), up approximately 20%. The two companies have a consistent year-on-year growth with no decline for 3 years.
- **Profitability trend:** The profitability trend of the two firms was high over the three years. However, Rightmove's operating margin (68%-75%) was greater than Auto Trader's (55%-63%) over the period.
- **Balance sheet strength:** The strength of the balance sheet of these companies was that they had little or no debt. All 3 years Rightmove had no borrowings and Auto Trader reduced its debt from £57.5m in 2023 to £0m in 2025. We note the repayment of outstanding borrowings (debt-to-equity falling from 0.11 to 0.00 over this period).
## Key Observations
1. The margin gap is shrinking fast, not staying the same. Rightmove had the higher operating margin every year studied, but the lead has dropped a lot, from 75% vs 55% in 2023 (a 20 point gap) to 68% vs 63% in 2025 (just 5 points). Rightmove is still ahead for now but Auto Trader has closed most of that gap in two years, which raises the question of whether that's from Auto Trader improving or Rightmove losing some of its edge.
2. Both firms are either working towards or maintaining a debt-free status. By 2025, Auto Trader paid off all its borrowings while Rightmove owned no debt. It is uncommon for businesses of this scale, which indicates a strong self-sustaining cash flow instead of dependence on financing.
3. The current ratio of right move is declining which requires monitoring. The drop from 2.41 in 2023 to 1.89 in 2025 is still comfortable above the 1.0 mark. Nonetheless, the downward trend is something to be flagged for future periods just note it's not a concern.
 ## Competitor Comparison  
Despite running in a similar, asset-light, subscription-based platform model, Rightmove is shown to be more profitable than its rival on all profitability metrics. Rightmove's operating margin advantage over Auto Trader has narrowed over the years, from a 20-percentage point lead in 2023 to just a 5-point lead in 2025, suggesting a narrowing profitability gap. Throughout the years, the current ratio has been stronger for Rightmove, showing better short-term financial health.
Auto Trader produces a larger absolute revenue base (£601.1m versus Rightmove's £425.1m in 2025) and shows the greater improvement in its balance sheet, which changed from being modestly in debt to being fully debt-free over the three-year period. By contrast, Rightmove has always been more or less debt-free.
It is worth noting that Auto Trader has a financial year which runs to 31 March and Rightmove to 31 December; the figures compared cannot thus be seen to be perfectly aligned in timing but both represent a full 12-month reporting period.
## Conclusion
Overall, both companies are financially strong, with consistent profit growth each year and minimal reliance on debt, Rightmove was debt-free throughout the period, while Auto Trader became fully debt-free by 2025. Despite Auto Trader's stronger balance sheet improvement and larger revenue base, Rightmove has been the more efficient and profitable of the two, with an operating margin advantage that, while narrowing from 20% in 2023 to 5% by 2025, held in Rightmove's favour throughout. One area worth investigating further is the split between core subscription revenue and any additional advertising or premium placement revenue, which could show whether margin strength comes from the core listing model or optional services.
